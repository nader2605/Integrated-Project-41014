Overview

A simulated TurtleBot3 Waffle Pi drives through a room with obstacles and docks at an ArUco-marked charging station. Wheel encoders and a gyro predict the pose, and the camera corrects it with an extended Kalman filter (EKF). The LiDAR planner and controllers only ever use the estimated state. Ground truth is kept for the simulator and for checking results.

It's a simulation-only Track A project built in MuJoCo, so there's no physical hardware. Our first pipeline passed 4 of 8 defined trials. After revising the sensing and control, the final pipeline passed 8 of 8 with final position errors of 5.6 to 8.2 cm and zero detected contacts. Those are small, development-used test samples though, so we don't claim general reliability.

The camera mast, 72-ray planar scanner, gyro, motor tuning and smooth tyre collision proxies are our adaptations of the ROBOTIS TurtleBot3 model (license kept in vendor/robotis_tb3). No ROS or robot connection is needed.

1. Modelling

The plant is simulated by MuJoCo (2 ms physics step, 50 Hz outer loop). The controller uses a reduced planar kinematic model with state \(x=[p_x,p_y,\theta]^T\) and input \(u=[v,\omega]^T\):

$$\dot x=[v\cos\theta,\ v\sin\theta,\ \omega]^T,\qquad \omega_{L,R}=\frac{v\mp b\omega/2}{r}$$

with wheel radius r = 0.033 m and wheel separation b = 0.288 m. Wheel speed is limited to ±7.88 rad/s and torque to ±0.2 N·m. Linearising about heading θ₀ and forward speed v₀ gives Ad = I + T·Ac, Bd = T·Bc.

  • Controllability: rank 3/3 at v₀ = 0.20 m/s. At v₀ = 0 the rank drops to 2, which is why we use nonzero-speed local tracking plus a manoeuvre-based docking controller.
  • Observability: rank 3/3 at the tested point with the landmark visible. A single range/bearing measurement alone has rank 2 at most, so we add camera-derived heading.

These are local ranks at stated operating points, not global claims.

2. Sensing

  • Camera: 640×480, 60° vertical FOV, so fx = fy = 415.69 px, principal point (320, 240), no distortion. It sits at (0.18, 0, 0.55) m on the base. This is simulator-derived calibration, not a real checkerboard calibration.
  • ArUco: marker ID 0, DICT_4X4_50. The black square is 0.68 × 700/980 = 0.4857 m. Using the white-bordered size would bias range. Pose comes from solvePnP with SOLVEPNP_IPPE_SQUARE. Non-finite PnP results are rejected.
  • Encoders and gyro: wheel joint-angle changes give travel. The gyro (zero bias assumed) gives a yaw increment, because wheel-difference yaw drifted when the tyres slipped during turns.
  • LiDAR: 72 rays over 360°, used below 4 m. It's idealised and has no range-noise test.

3. Estimation (EKF)

Prediction uses mean wheel travel Δs and the gyro increment Δθg:

$$\hat x^-_{k+1}=\hat x_k+[\Delta s\cos\theta_m,\ \Delta s\sin\theta_m,\ \Delta\theta_g]^T,\quad \theta_m=\hat\theta_k+\Delta\theta_g/2$$

The camera gives range, bearing and heading of the known panel at (2.391, 0) m, with the 0.18 m camera offset included in the model:

$$h(x)=[\,r,\ \mathrm{wrap}(\mathrm{atan2}(d_y,d_x)-\theta),\ \theta\,]^T,\quad R=\mathrm{diag}(0.05^2,(2^\circ)^2,(8^\circ)^2)$$

Updates are gated by Mahalanobis distance (11.34 for three components) and use the Joseph covariance form. Initial covariance is P₀ = diag(0.03, 0.03, (5°)²) and the spawn pose is known. Across the final eight trials, position RMSE was 0.0354 to 0.0627 m (unweighted mean 0.0449 m).

4. Planning and control

  • Planner: each LiDAR scan is placed in a temporary 0.10 m grid (8 m × 8 m) using the EKF pose. Obstacles are inflated to 0.50 m (0.40 m footprint + 0.10 m margin) and eight-connected A* heads for the pre-dock point (0.95, 0). It isn't SLAM, and unseen space counts as free.
  • Waypoint commitment: the robot keeps a waypoint until it's within 0.16 m or it's blocked. This fixed an oscillation where replanning picked opposite sides of an obstacle.
  • LQR tracker: Q = diag(7, 7, 2.5), R = diag(1, 0.55), speed capped at 0.16 m/s. If heading error is above 0.22 rad it turns in place first.
  • Footprint guard: vlim = max(0, min(0.16, 0.6(d − 0.08))) based on current scan returns.
  • Docking: state machine of Navigate, Acquire, Approach, Reacquire, Realign and Docked. DOCKED needs 15 consecutive steps with an accepted camera update, estimated distance ≤ 0.08 m, heading < 8°, marker bearing < 8° and range within 0.12 m of 0.661 m.

Experiments and results

Four conditions, seeds 7 and 19, each with a 140 simulated-second limit:

  • Baseline: known spawn (−2.05, −1.35, 0), nominal noise.
  • Start pose: x ∈ [−2.25, −1.85], y ∈ [−1.65, −1.10], yaw ±15° (known to the estimator).
  • High noise: sensor noise doubled, EKF covariance matched.
  • Push: 2 N sideways force for 0.3 s starting at 12 s.

A trial passes only if the controller docks, actual position error ≤ 0.15 m, actual heading error ≤ 15°, and no contact is detected (checked every physics step). Ground truth is used only to judge this.

Both initial start-pose trials hit the orange obstacle and timed out, push seed 19 also hit it, and push seed 7 timed out 0.406 m from the target. We kept all of those failures in the repo. Mean revised docking error was 0.0694 m and mean completion time was 73.1 s.

Trade-off: in the two baseline trials mean time went from 49.1 s to 75.9 s (about 55% slower) while mean error dropped from 0.0937 m to 0.0665 m. The revised version isn't better on every metric.

Estimate with uncertainty

The live demo (demo.py) shows the estimated pose, the true pose, an orange joint 95% position ellipse and marginal ±2σ values during the push trial. The ellipse and ±2σ are different confidence conventions and neither guarantees the real error stays inside. Push seed 7 was re-run with covariance logging and reproduced 84.26 s, 0.0642 m error and zero contacts.

Windows verification

The first Windows run passed 6/8. Two trials (start pose and high noise, seed 19) hit non-finite values right after a camera EKF update, because the detector only checked translation. We made the detector reject non-finite rotation, translation and panel normals, and made the EKF reject non-finite measurements. No gains or thresholds changed. The rerun passed 8/8 with errors of 5.8 to 8.3 cm and times of 60.2 to 84.7 s. We kept the original 6/8 run.

Limitations

  • Only two seeds per condition in one static room, and they were used during development. This isn't a held-out test or a success-rate estimate.
  • The comparison covers the whole revised stack (360° scan, planner, gyro prediction, marker latch, realignment), not each change on its own.
  • Starting pose, marker location, panel orientation and camera mount are known to the estimator.
  • Gyro bias, camera dropout or blur, LiDAR noise and covariance mismatch aren't tested.
  • The macOS comparison and the Windows run use different builds, so it's not a like-for-like platform benchmark.
  • No hardware, charging mechanism or sim-to-real comparison.
